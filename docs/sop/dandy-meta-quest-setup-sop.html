Meet Dandy Meta Quest Setup

Standard Operating Procedure

Lab Tour in a BoxMeta Quest 3Version 1.0 · October 2026

What You’ll Need

  • Your Dandy Lab Tour in a Box kit: the Meta Quest 3 headset, two Touch Plus controllers, the USB‑C charging cable and power adapter, a Dandy Fusion Denture, and the access card with your six‑digit Dandy code.
  • A smartphone (iPhone or Android) with Bluetooth turned on, to run the free Meta Horizon app during first‑time setup.
  • A Wi‑Fi network and its password. Home or office Wi‑Fi works best. Guest networks that open a sign‑in page (hotels, some conference centers) may not connect; a phone hotspot is a reliable fallback.
  • A Meta account (free). You can create one during setup with just an email address — no Facebook account is required. You must be 13 or older.
  • About 10 minutes and a clear space of at least 3 ft × 3 ft. The lab tour is designed to be watched seated or standing in place.

Before You Start

  1. Charge the headset. Plug the included USB‑C cable into the port on the left side of the headset and into the power adapter. The side LED is orange while charging and green when full; a full charge takes about 2½ hours. Setup includes a software update, so keep the headset plugged in until it finishes.
  2. Unwrap. Peel the protective film off both lenses and remove the paper from the head strap.
  3. Wake the controllers. Pull the paper battery tab out of each controller. Each one runs on a single AA battery, already installed.
  4. Keep the lenses out of direct sunlight. Sunlight focused through the lenses permanently damages the display. Store the headset in its box, away from windows.

1.Set Up Your Meta Account and Headset

On your phone

  1. Install the Meta Horizon app from the Apple App Store or Google Play.
  2. Open the app and Log in, or choose Create account and sign up with your email address and birthday. When prompted, pick a display name and username for your Meta Horizon profile.
  3. Make sure Bluetooth is on (and, on Android, that nearby‑device sharing is allowed). The app uses it to find the headset.

On the headset

  1. Power on. Press and hold the power button on the left side for about three seconds, until you hear a chime and see the Meta logo.
  2. Put it on and adjust the fit. Loosen the back and top straps, seat the headset against your face, then tighten the split straps and the top strap so the weight sits on the top of your head rather than your cheeks. Turn the lens‑spacing wheel on the underside of the headset until the picture is sharp.
  3. Pair the controllers. Hold the Menu button on the left controller and the Meta button on the right controller for about two seconds. A short buzz and a blinking white light mean they are connected. Slip the wrist straps on.
  4. Choose your language and connect to Wi‑Fi. Point the controller at your network, press the trigger to select it, and type the password on the floating keyboard.
  5. Pair the headset with your phone. The headset displays a five‑digit pairing code. In the Meta Horizon app, tap Menu → Devices → Pair new headset → Meta Quest 3, then enter the code when asked. The phone finishes signing the headset into your Meta account.
  6. Let the update install. The headset downloads and installs its latest software. Allow 5–15 minutes and keep it plugged in. The Store and Library only appear once this finishes.
  7. Create your boundary. The first time you enter VR you are asked to set a boundary. Choose Stationary — it creates a 3 ft × 3 ft area around where you are standing or sitting, which is all the lab tour needs. Confirm the floor level if prompted.
Tip: The phone app is only needed for this first setup. Everyone who uses the headset afterwards can skip straight to step 2. If you don’t see the option to add a headset in the app, update the app, fully close it, and reopen it.

2.Download Immersa from the Horizon Store

  1. Press the Meta button on the right controller to open the Navigator, the menu bar that appears in front of you.
  2. Select the Meta Horizon Store (the shopping‑bag icon).
  3. Select the search icon at the top of the Store, type Immersa, and select it from the results.
  4. Select Get. The app is free; it downloads and installs automatically.
  5. When the button changes to Open, select it. From now on you’ll also find Immersa in your Library (the grid icon in the Navigator).
Tip: If the Library looks empty after installing, the headset may be signed in to a different Meta Horizon profile. Open Settings → Accounts and switch back to the profile you created in step 1.

3.Enter Your Six‑Digit Dandy Code

  1. Open Immersa.
  2. When Immersa asks for an access code, enter the six digits printed on the card in your kit. If you scanned the card’s QR code, the same code is shown on the page it opened (lp.meetdandy.com/quest).
  3. Point the controller at each digit and press the trigger, then select Continue.
Tip: Codes are specific to your kit. If the card is missing or the code is rejected, contact your Dandy representative and we’ll issue a new one.

4.Open the Dandy Experience

  1. Once the code is accepted you land in the dedicated Dandy space inside Immersa.
  2. Select the Dandy Lab Tour to open it.
  3. Get comfortable. If the picture isn’t centered in front of you, hold the Meta button on the right controller for a second to recenter the view. Volume buttons are on the underside of the headset, on the right.

5.Stream, Play or Download

  • Stream plays the tour immediately over Wi‑Fi. Use it when you have a solid connection and want to start right away.
  • Download saves the tour to the headset so it plays anywhere, with no Wi‑Fi. We recommend downloading before you pass the headset around the office or take it to an event.
  • Play starts the tour. Point and press the trigger to pause, resume, or skip. Hold the Meta button to recenter at any time.
  • To exit, press the Meta button and select Quit, or simply take the headset off — it goes to sleep on its own.

Sharing the Headset with Your Team

  • Hand‑off checklist: wipe the facial interface, hand the headset over with the controllers, have the next person adjust the straps and the lens‑spacing wheel, and open Immersa → Dandy Lab Tour. If Immersa asks for the code again, re‑enter the code from your card.
  • Cleaning: use non‑abrasive antibacterial wipes on the straps and facial interface (no alcohol or abrasive cleaners). Clean the lenses and the front camera glass with a dry optical microfiber cloth only.
  • Battery: the headset runs for roughly two hours of use. Plug it in between sessions so it’s ready for the next person.
  • Then share the real thing: when your team has seen the lab, book a live tour or talk to the Dandy team from lp.meetdandy.com/quest.

Troubleshooting

ProblemTry this
The headset won’t turn onCharge it for at least 30 minutes with the included cable and adapter (18 W or higher), then hold the power button for three seconds.
The pairing code never appeared, or setup was already completedOn the headset, open Settings → System → About to see the pairing code. Make sure the phone’s Bluetooth is on and that the phone and headset are on the same Wi‑Fi network.
Controllers won’t pairHold Menu (left) and Meta (right) for two seconds. Replace the AA battery if the light never comes on. To factory‑reset a controller: right — hold A and tap Meta five times; left — hold X and tap Menu five times. The light blinks orange/white, then turns light blue.
Wi‑Fi won’t connectForget the network and join it again, or try the 2.4 GHz band. Restart the headset (hold power → Restart). Networks with a sign‑in page may not work — use a phone hotspot instead.
The picture is blurryReseat the headset on your face and retighten the straps, then turn the lens‑spacing wheel slowly until text is sharp. If you wear glasses, keep them on.
A boundary grid keeps appearingPress Meta → Quick controls → Boundary and switch to Stationary, or clear the area around you and redraw it.
Immersa isn’t in the Store searchCheck the spelling, then make sure the headset update has finished: Settings → System → Software update.
The code is rejectedRe‑enter the six digits from the card carefully. If it still fails, contact your Dandy representative for a replacement code.
The update is stuckKeep the headset plugged in and give it 15 minutes. If nothing changes, hold the power button and choose Restart.
