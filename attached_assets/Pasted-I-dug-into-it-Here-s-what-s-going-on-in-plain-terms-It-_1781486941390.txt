I dug into it. Here's what's going on, in plain terms.

It is firing — but only in a very narrow way, which is why it looks dead. Right now the page CTA only fills in buttons that have no link of their own. The catch: almost every block comes pre-loaded with its own button (a label like "Get started" and a link), and AI-generated pages bake one into each block too. So the block's own button always wins, and your page CTA never gets a chance to show up. The plumbing all works — page setting saves, reaches the page, reaches the buttons — it's just being masked everywhere.

That's the opposite of what you described wanting. You want the page CTA to be the one source of truth that drives every button, with individual blocks able to override it.

To make it behave that way, here's what I'd change:

When you turn the page CTA on, it becomes the default for every button on the page — overriding the labels/links the blocks shipped with.
Add a small "use a custom button here" switch on individual blocks, so you can opt a specific block out and give it its own button.
Leave any page where the page CTA is turned off exactly as it is today — so nothing on your existing live pages changes unless you opt in.
The one tradeoff worth flagging: on a page where you switch the page CTA on, the buttons that blocks came with will be replaced by your page CTA (that's the point) — and for any block you'd previously hand-tweaked, you'd flip its "custom button" switch to keep it.
