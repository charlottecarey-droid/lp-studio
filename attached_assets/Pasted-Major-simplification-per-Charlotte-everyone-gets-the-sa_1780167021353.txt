For you, Charlotte — two strategic notes:
The most consequential single piece of conversion architecture here is the "what you'd keep / what you'd lose" panel. That's the difference between "trial expired, I'll figure it out next month" and "trial expired and I'm losing the Sales Console I just set up — let me upgrade today." Make sure the implementation shows specifics (their actual page count, their actual Salesforce connection status, their actual brand setup) rather than generic feature-list strings. Concreteness drives conversion.
The "every upgrade CTA mentions both Growth and Scale" point is worth holding the line on, even though it adds copy length to every prompt. Default behavior in B2B SaaS is users self-select to the trial tier — so by anchoring every upgrade CTA to Growth alone, you lock yourself into a one-tier ARPU. Mentioning Scale in the upgrade flow ("Or unlock AI image gen + multi-workspace with Scale →") raises ARPU on the customers who would have paid more if asked. It's a small copy change with real revenue implications. Worth designing this into the upgrade-prompt component from day one rather than retrofitting it later.
