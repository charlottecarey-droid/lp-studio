# Replit prompt — broaden CampaignsScene to show send-direct + push-to-MAP story

The CampaignsScene on the homepage (`artifacts/lp-studio/src/marketing/components/CampaignsScene.tsx`) currently tells a single story: build a campaign in LPS, send directly via Resend, watch the identity reveal happen. That's only half the actual product story.

The full story is **dual-channel**: either

1. **Send directly from LPS** via Resend (the current story), OR
2. **Push the campaign list + per-recipient tokenized URLs** to the user's existing send stack — Marketo, HubSpot, Salesforce, Google Sheets, or any webhook — and let their MAP/CRM/sequencer do the actual send.

The identity reveal (the wedge) works the same either way, because the tokenized URLs are baked at list-build time, not at send time. That's worth saying explicitly because it's what makes LPS pluggable into existing GTM stacks rather than a replacement for them.

Make the section tell that dual story without losing the existing visual.
