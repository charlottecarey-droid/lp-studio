Design Direction (Important)


Overall style: Modern, minimal, Apple-inspired. Clean, confident, not cluttered


Spacing: Generous whitespace, clear section separation, strong visual hierarchy


Typography: Large, bold headlines paired with minimal supporting copy (avoid long paragraphs)


Color: Use restrained palettes with occasional high-contrast sections for emphasis


Imagery: High-quality, editorial-style visuals or product-focused UI—not generic stock



Motion & Interaction


Subtle, smooth animations (no overuse)


Scroll-based storytelling (especially with sticky stack sections)


Light parallax or depth where appropriate


Hover states that feel responsive and intentional


Avoid anything that feels gimmicky or distracting



Component Quality Bar


Each section/block should feel like it could stand alone as a premium component


Avoid filler sections or repeated patterns across templates


Every block should have a clear purpose (conversion, storytelling, or proof)



Important
