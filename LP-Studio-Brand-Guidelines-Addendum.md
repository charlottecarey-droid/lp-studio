# LP Studio — Brand Guidelines Addendum
### Positioning, growth & content consistency · June 2026

Your Brand Guidelines v1.0 are strong and, importantly, **already horizontal** — "the AI
revenue workspace" for marketing and sales, between Mutiny / Webflow / Unbounce. This
addendum reconciles them with the product roadmap and the organic growth plan, and locks
in the experiment-led positioning you asked for. Nothing here contradicts v1.0; it extends
it. Fold these into v1.1 when you next revise the deck.

## 1. Positioning: horizontal, experiment-led (not vertical-committed)

- **The brand stays horizontal.** Keep "the AI revenue workspace" / "describe a page, watch
  it build" as the top-line promise. Do **not** rebrand around dentistry or any single
  vertical right now.
- **Dental/DSO is a proof point, not the positioning.** Where the guidelines use a dental
  example (e.g. "Acme Dental" in the welcome email), treat it as *one illustrative
  customer*, and vary the examples — show an event, a SaaS launch, an agency, a local
  service alongside it, so the brand reads as horizontal. Add a line to the Brand Story:
  *"Vertical-agnostic by design — the same canvas works for any revenue team."*
- **Let the market name the niche.** Messaging should invite many use-cases in; the funnel
  (not the brand) decides where to lean. Commit a vertical only when the data proves one.

## 2. The voice already IS the growth strategy

Your voice principles — confident, plainspoken, allergic to fluff, answer-first, real
mechanics, parallel triads — are *exactly* what AI answer engines (GEO) reward and what
ranks. No new "content voice" is needed. Apply the existing voice to the blog verbatim:

- **Lead with the answer.** The "concrete over abstract" principle becomes a GEO rule:
  the first two sentences of every post answer the title. (AI engines cite the opening.)
- **Keep the Say/Don't list.** "Revolutionary / seamless / supercharge," invented stats,
  and fake logos are banned in blog copy too — doubly so, since "radically honest" is a
  brand pillar *and* a trust differentiator AI buyers care about.
- **Sentence-case headlines, mono kickers.** Blog post titles and section markers follow
  the same casing + the numbered-mono-marker device, not pill eyebrows.

## 3. Content & blog styling (consistency rules)

The blog must look unmistakably LP Studio:

- **Canvas + cards:** cream `#F6F2E9` background, white cards with ink-hairline borders,
  ink `#1A1815` text, ~720px reading measure for articles, ~1180px for the index.
- **Type:** DM Sans for post titles/headings, Inter for body (16–17px / 1.6), JetBrains
  Mono for kickers (tag · date · reading time).
- **Color discipline:** indigo `#4B47E5` for links/primary actions; **coral `#E26B4F`
  only as a spark** (a single accent, a "live"/winner mark) — never a base or a flood.
  Legacy lime stays retired.
- **Infographics:** brand-colored inline SVG (cream/ink/indigo, coral spark for one
  emphasis), Lucide icons at 2px, warm/real photography — never cool/sterile stock, never
  rainbow gradients. Generous whitespace; let it breathe.
- **CTA:** every post ends in one clear, imperative CTA — "Create your workspace" /
  "Describe a page. Watch it build." — never stacked competing CTAs.

## 4. Product ↔ brand ↔ growth consistency

These three now tell one story:

- **Product** generates on-brand pages and now publishes its *own* on-brand blog from the
  same system — the marketing site is the proof the product works.
- **Brand** is horizontal, honest, fast — and that honesty ("no fake stats, ever") is also
  the trust pitch for the conversational AI and the GEO content strategy.
- **Growth** is content + product-led, in the brand's own voice, building citable authority
  while staying vertical-agnostic so the market can reveal the wedge.
